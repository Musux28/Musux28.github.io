~/whoami ■ the person behind the brain

Graduated in Italy. Came to the U.S. to build an ambitious career in cybersecurity. Ready.

I grew up in Giarre, a small town at the foot of Mount Etna. I earned my B.S. in Electronics and Computer Engineering in Italy, where I learned how hardware and software actually talk to each other, and where I first started asking what happens when someone makes them talk in ways they shouldn't.

Before moving to the U.S., I worked in my parents' ceramics and home-renovation showroom. A family business teaches what no course does: listening to customers, coordinating suppliers and installers, and being the one people count on when something goes wrong. That is where I learned to work in a team, and to stay calm when a problem lands on your desk at the worst moment. It turns out that is half of incident response.

In 2025 I moved to Albany for an M.S. in Cybersecurity at the University at Albany, and joined NSA-sponsored research on formal verification and cryptography. I graduate in December 2026.

Why a brain?

This site is my way of showing how I think. Security has two halves: the attacker's curiosity and the defender's discipline. Every project here sits in one hemisphere, and the research that connects them sits in the middle. Each one is something I built, broke, or proved, and each one is something I genuinely enjoyed doing.
